# Submission checklist

- [ ] G4 has explicit human approval.
- [ ] Journal requirements were re-verified against official sources.
- [ ] Manuscript content derives from the approved English master.
- [ ] References resolve in both directions.
- [ ] Required statements and files are present.
- [ ] Blind manuscript contains no identity or hidden author metadata.
- [ ] Comments, tracked changes, placeholders, and internal notes are absent.
- [ ] DOCX structural audit passed.
- [ ] Every final DOCX page was rendered and visually inspected.
- [ ] Submission filenames match the journal rules.
- [ ] The final submission bundle has a manifest and SHA-256 hashes.
